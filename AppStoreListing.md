## Description (4000 char max)

```
Gratitude is a simple twice-a-day habit: a short check-in when you wake up, another before bed.

Tell it how much time you have and it adjusts the questions. Some mornings it's a couple of taps, other times you actually write something out. The evening questions build on whatever you said that morning, so the two aren't disconnected.

Whenever you want, open Reflect and it pulls up one day from your past, morning and evening together, with a photo if you added one. Just one memory at a time, not a feed to scroll through.

If a check-in reminds you of someone, you can tag them and send a smile, which is really just a text message with a link. They don't need the app. Reflect can send that same one again later with a tap if it comes back up.

There's a streak, but it only counts a day once both check-ins are done, and missing a day just quietly resets it rather than making a thing of it.

Everything is stored on your phone first. If you turn on iCloud backup, it's encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere, so we can't read it either. You can export or delete all of it whenever you want, no questions asked.
```
